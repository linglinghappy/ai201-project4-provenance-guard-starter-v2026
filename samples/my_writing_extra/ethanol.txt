The reading article implies three reasons that Ethanol fuel is not a good replacement for gasoline. However, the lecturer thinks the points made in the reading article are not convincing.

First, the author of the article indicates that the increasing use of ethanol fuel would not solve the environmental problem of global warming. Nevertheless. the speaker states that the rising use of ethanol fuel will not aggregate global warming. Although burning them releases carbon dioxide into the atmosphere, the process of growing plants can counteract the emitting of carbon dioxide. This is because when plants are growing, they absorb carbon dioxide which actually removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

Second, the reading illustrates that the large-scale production of ethanol reduces the amount of plants available for feeding animals. Nonetheless, the lecturer opposes the claim by pointing out that ethanol is produced by the part of plants that are not used to feed animals. As a result, the amount of plants serving as the food source for animals will not disappear.

Third, the reading mentions that the price of ethanol fuel will never compete with gasoline. The reason why the price of both ethanol fuel and gasoline are the same is because the tax subsidies from the United States government decrease the cost of ethanol fuel. Yet the speaker contends that in the future the price of them will be the same in terms of the costs. This is because once there are more people buying ethanol fuel, the production will increase as well which will lead to a drop in the price of it. In addition, he also maintains that when the production of ethanol fuel increases three times, the cost will decrease up to 40 percent.
