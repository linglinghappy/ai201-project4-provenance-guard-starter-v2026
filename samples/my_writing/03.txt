I disagree with the statement that scientists and scholars should concentrate on the research area that may be beneficial for the majority of the public. From my perspective, these experts should contribute themselves in the area that they are passionate about, in this way, they can continue to engage in a specific research area and bring advancement to the improvement of society.

I believe that researchers are more motivated and delighted when they work in the research area where they are interested and even though the process of the experiments is frustrating and tedious, they can still persist in their work. For example, my elder sister is a horticulture PhD student and she was struggling with the experiment of planting cabbage. Her goal of the research was to provide solutions for the cultivation of healthy cabbage in the Taiwanese agriculture industry. She was heartbroken when her experimental plants died or the cells in the dishes did not grow. Nevertheless, her passion led her to adjust her research steps and methods and eventually, she completed the work that helped the local farmers.

Some people may claim that scientists and researchers are responsible for enhancing the largest number of people’s lives because they have more resources and abilities than others. However, scientists who dedicated themselves to a topic that only helps a minority of humans can still be a great accomplishment. For instance, some scientists working on combining artificial intelligence techniques and medical knowledge in order to provide a better model to assist doctors in detecting cancer. This work saves people's lives because it enables patients to get adequate treatment in the early stages of cancer. Thus, once the scientists or researchers find their call and explore their chosen study field they are willing to contribute, they are all valuable no matter what the number of people that the research work benefits.

In conclusion, it is important for scientists and researchers to commit themselves to the area that they are interested in because they can be more persistent and motivated in their work. In addition, though the number of people that a research benefit is crucial, how and in what aspect the work changes and improves one’s life is also essential regarding the impact of their research commitment.
