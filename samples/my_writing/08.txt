Both Rachel’s and Mike’s opinions on allocating university resources are reasonable. From my perspective, I contend that spending a budget on both academic facilities and sports programs is the best strategy for universities to get high academic rankings and increase revenue. My university, National Chengchi University, was fully committed to building a brand-new library called Dah Hsian Seetoo Library. Students gather there to self-study and faculties organize academic activities to interact with professionals. As a result, the learning hub improves the learning environment and enhances the university’s academic performance. In addition, the university launched a basketball team, the ‘NCCU Griffins', in 2017. They then won three successive championships at the university basketball association. Therefore, they brought the alumni community together and attracted more donations. Overall, striking a balance between academic facilities and athletic events is essential for the university.
