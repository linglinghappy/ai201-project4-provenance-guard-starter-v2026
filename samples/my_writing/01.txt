Steve Job was proud of giving speech for graduates of Stanford. For the purpose of encourage students who were going to join the work place, he shared three stories from his life. 
First of all, he told a story about connecting the dots. Considering that spending a large amount of money on college fee was unvalued, he decided to drop out. Although he experienced a tough time, he found out that following his own curiosity and intuition was priceless. To illustrate, the typography he learned in a calligraphy class turned out to be the beautiful front in Mac. He believed that everything you’ve done may become the fruit of your life.  
Furthermore, he shared about love and loss. Job became unemployed after being kicking off by the company he founded, Apple. Convincing the faith , he kept doing what he loved. Consequently, he returned to Apple with applause. “Keep searching, don’t compromise.” “Do what you love and love what you do.” he indicated.
Last but not least, he discussed about death. Jobs realized how precious the life is after being diagnosed with cancer. Living up to other’s expectation will waste our life. The inner voice should be the top priority.
At the end, Steve Job wished all graduates be courage for the adventure in the future and keep the idiom” Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish” in mind!
