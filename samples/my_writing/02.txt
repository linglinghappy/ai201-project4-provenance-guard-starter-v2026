Business English: Communication Skills & Negotiation Strategies is an interesting and challenging course. Besides, I had a lot of fun learning English through the participation education. It was a wonderful experience to learn acting like a professional business people by all kinds of different mock conference practice. The course provided student a precious opportunity to utilize knowledge in the business world and integrate theory and practice.

Wearing makeup, suit, and high heels was a routine for us on Thursdays. This was indeed a unique feature of the courses because I have been asked for the question’ Ling, are you going to have an interview or something formal today?’ more than twenty times from my friends when I took another class. That means I really look difference on every Thursday and I successfully create a formal image. After explaining the reason, my friends thought the course was really cool and practical and they were curious about it. For me, I have learned how to cultivate my taste in clothing as one of a preparation to step in to the working field.

In terms of the course design, I liked the decent size of the class. It was great for students to interact each other in the 22-people class. In this case, I had more chances to recognize my classmates deeper and also learn from them. In addition, the classroom was fantastic and modern and that was one of the reasons why I was in a good mood while taking the class. Also, I appreciated for the “ Clareance sharing time.” Listening teacher sharing his own experience and stories dealing with people in the real business world was not only the best medicine for waking me up when I was almost falling asleep in class but also helped me to realize how does the business world operate. 

If I have to point out something that can be improved for the course, I would say the time. Taking class from 12:00 to 15:00 is something annoying for me because I was forces to sacrifice having a normal lunch and taking a nap. Then, I wished teacher to announce for all the details of the assignments or projects in the first class of the course so that we can started prepare for the whole semester. To be honest, there were lots of difficulties when all the activities were squeezed in the schedule after the midterm.

In conclusion, I am delightful for meeting Clareance and new friends in class and I believe I will miss people and the Thursday happy afternoon time.
