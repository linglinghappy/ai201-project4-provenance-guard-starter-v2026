I support Mark’s idea that social media has a positive effect on society. From my perspective, I think social media services enhance the quality of people’s lives when we utilize them properly. Social media gives students access to more educational resources. For example, I learn English by reading the posts of vocabulary from an Instagram account called ‘The English Island.’ In addition, since there are a lot of free videos on YouTube, people can subscribe to YouTubers from different professional fields in order to gain more knowledge. To elaborate, by watching videos from a dermatologist channel, people can recognize their skin type and make wiser decisions when purchasing skin products. Moreover, media platforms, where citizens discuss their points of view on various issues, play a significant role when the government makes a policy. When people complained that masks were totally sold out on PPT and Facebook during the COVID pandemic, Taiwan’s government built up the national mask manufacturers and launched an app to provide people with information on how and where to get medical supplies. Overall, social media has a more beneficial impact on our society.
