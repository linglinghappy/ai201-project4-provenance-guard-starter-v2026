The reading  discusses the "let it burn" policy that caused three kinds of damages  after the forest fires in the summer of 1988 in Yellowstone . However the lecturer states those points are questionable. She implies that  forest fires are a natural part of the ecological system and have a positive effect on the environment.


First of all, the reading passage mentions that park's tree and other vegetation are damaged by the Yellowstone fires. Yet, the lecturer contends that the fires promoted the diversity of the plant since the land will be colonized by new plants. For instance, seeds that are only generated in high temperatures will grow newly.


Secondly, the writer  indicates that the wildlife of Yellowstone were endangered by the fire. However, the lecturer states that the population of the wildlife will recover. Because once the smaller plants replace the damaged land, it becomes an ideal habitat for some small animals such as rabbits and hares. Then,  the predators of those small animals will have more food. As a result, the food chain of the wildlife gets stronger.


Thirdly, the reading reveals that the local economy is harmed by the Yellowstone fire. Nevertheless, the speaker argues that the forest fire does not happen every year and the fire is caused by massive factors. In addition,  forest fires have not occurred after the 1988 Yellowstone fire. Eventually. visitors come back and the tourism of Yellowstone revives.
