I support Kelly's idea that watching television makes people physically unhealthy because they are not moving around. From my perspective, I think that it makes the audience mentally unhealthy. Take my country, Taiwan, as an example. Taiwan's media are the origin of the chaos in society. They always report biased political content, news of car accidents, and even fake news. To be honest, I feel disappointed about the future of my country after watching television. In addition, by feeding people meaningless and negative information, we will become hopeless and lack motivation to improve the quality of our lives. Overall, I believe that passively obtaining information from television is harmful to people's mental health.
