I support Maria’s idea of investing the city's budget in infrastructure. In my opinion, improving the overall quality of life for people means making people’s lives more convenient with a lower cost of living. Spending the government budget on public transportation can achieve the goal. Take my city, Taichung, as an example. When I was a high school student, the city government launched a 'Free local city bus' policy. As a commuter, I spend zero dollars on the cost of transportation. In addition, because using the public transportation system could save money, more and more people were willing to take the buses instead of driving their own private cars. As a result, the severe traffic jam problems were solved. Eventually, since the city residents became used to utilizing the public transport service, the city government decided to build a metro. Consequently, investing city spending in public transportation allows people to lower their living expenses, which is a significant strategy to enhance the quality of life for people.
